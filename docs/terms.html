2. The service

screensnap records a browser tab, an app window, or your whole screen (with optional webcam and microphone), captures screenshots, and edits the result in a built-in editor, exporting MP4 or GIF. All capture, editing, and encoding run locally on your device. screensnap is provided free of charge, with no account, no time limits, and no watermarks.
