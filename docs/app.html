What it does

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Capture and edit in one place — and it never phones home.

Record anything

Current tab, a whole screen, or any app window. Add a floating camera + controls bubble (Screen + Cam), or record just the webcam.

Screenshots + annotate

Visible area or full-page capture, then arrows, text, highlight, blur/redact, crop, layers and a beautify backdrop — saved as a clean PNG.

A real video editor

Trim, cut out mistakes (multi-segment), free-form or ratio crop, keyframed zoom blocks, animated GIF & image overlays — live preview, WYSIWYG.

Beautify & zoom

Wrap a clip in a gradient backdrop with rounded corners and a soft shadow, and zoom into the action with draggable, resizable focus blocks.

Export, your way

Save the original instantly, or export an edited native MP4 (no transcode) or an animated GIF — all encoded on your device.

Private by design

No servers, no telemetry, no analytics, no runtime network calls. Captures stay on your machine. Free and open source (GPL-3.0).

How it works

Capture → edit → export

  1. 1
    CapturePick a screenshot or a recording source. A finished recording opens straight in the editor.
  2. 2
    EditTrim and cut, crop, add zoom and a backdrop, drop in annotations, logos or a looping GIF.
  3. 3
    ExportDownload the native MP4 or an animated GIF — encoded locally, watermark-free.
Privacy-first

Your screen never leaves your screen

Everything — capture, the webcam bubble, annotation, trimming, encoding and saving — happens entirely in your browser, on your machine.
