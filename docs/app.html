SIX THINGS IT DOES WELL
01

Record anything

Current tab, a whole screen, or any app window. Add a floating camera + controls bubble, or capture just the webcam.

02

Screenshots & annotate

Visible-area or full-page capture, then arrows, text, highlight, blur/redact and crop — saved as a clean PNG.

03

A real video editor

A multi-track timeline: trim, cut, crop and zoom, time-scoped text / arrow / image / GIF overlays, audio mute regions and a voiceover track — live preview.

04

Beautify & zoom

Wrap a clip in a gradient backdrop with rounded corners and a soft shadow, then zoom in with draggable, resizable focus blocks.

05

Export your way

Save the original instantly, or export an edited native MP4 (720p / 1080p presets that genuinely shrink files) or an animated GIF — all encoded on your device.

06

Private by design

No servers, no telemetry, no analytics, no runtime network calls. Captures stay on your machine. Free and open source (GPL-3.0).

HOW IT WORKS

Capture → edit → export

01

Capture

Pick a screenshot or a recording source. A finished recording opens straight in the editor.

02

Edit

Trim, cut and zoom on a multi-track timeline, mute or mix in audio, add a backdrop, annotations, logos or a looping GIF.

03

Export

Download a native MP4 or an animated GIF — encoded locally, watermark-free.

PRIVACY-FIRST · NO ACCOUNT · NO CLOUD

Your screen never leaves your screen.

Everything — capture, the webcam bubble, annotation, trimming, encoding and saving — happens entirely in your browser, on your machine. There is nothing to sign into and nowhere for your recordings to go.
