Screen recording shouldn't cost money, stamp a watermark on your video, or make you create an account. screensnap records your screen and captures screenshots completely free — no time limit, no watermark, no sign-up, no cloud. Everything stays on your machine. This product is 100% open source, feel free to open feature requests via github.

FREE, UNLIMITED RECORDING
• Record your whole screen, any app window, or a single browser tab — saved as native MP4 (H.264/AAC), with no time limit and no watermark, ever
• Screen + Cam: your webcam floats on top in an always-on-top bubble with pause and stop right on it — set it up first (circle or square, mirrored or not), then record
• System / tab audio and/or microphone, with pause, resume, and mic mute
• On-page and floating controls, plus global keyboard shortcuts (stop, pause, restart)
• Draw on the screen while you record (strokes auto-fade), restart a take instantly, or hide the camera on the fly
• No account, no upgrade prompts, no "pro" tier — it's all just free

BUILT-IN VIDEO EDITOR (ALSO FREE)
• Trim the ends, or cut mistakes out of the middle with multi-segment cuts
• Crop free-form or to a ratio (16:9, 1:1, 9:16, 4:3), and change resolution and speed
• Zoom into the action with draggable, resizable focus blocks that ease in and out
• Beautify: wrap the video in a gradient or solid backdrop with rounded corners and a soft shadow
• Add logos, images, animated GIFs, and annotations (text, arrows, shapes, blur) as layers
• Export a native MP4 or an animated GIF — or download the original clip untouched
• Everything is encoded on your own device — no transcoding servers, no watermark

SCREENSHOTS
• Visible-area and full-page screenshots (PNG)
• Annotate before saving: text, arrows, shapes, highlight, blur
• Crop, and manage everything as layers — reorder, hide, add image/logo layers, or go back and re-edit your text
• Beautify with a padded backdrop, then copy to clipboard or save to your computer

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• 100% local: capture, processing, and storage never leave your device
• No servers, no telemetry, no analytics — zero network calls
• Minimal permissions: screen and window sharing use Chrome's own picker, and screensnap only touches the tab you point it at — no background access to your browsing
