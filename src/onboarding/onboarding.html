screensnap.

Free screen recording & screenshots — 100% on your machine.

No account, no watermark, no cloud, no time limit. Everything you capture stays on your device.

Keep the recording controls with you across sites

Recordings show a small control bar (it auto-hides so it stays out of your video). To keep it working when you navigate to a different website mid-recording, screensnap needs permission to run on all sites. It's optional — skip it and we'll ask only if you need it. Either way, nothing is ever sent anywhere.

✓ Enabled — you're all set.
While recording, stop or pause from anywhere with Alt+Shift+S (stop) and Alt+Shift+P (pause). Move your pointer to the bottom-left corner to reveal the controls.

Open source · GPL-3.0 · No telemetry, no analytics, no network calls. You can change site access anytime from the extension's details page.
